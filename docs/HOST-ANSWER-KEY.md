# Host answer key: Operation QUEENBEE

> **Spoilers.** For the host and the content lead only. Never show this to players. If you change the case in the admin panel, this file no longer matches.

## The answer

- **Thief:** Sid “Overfit” Sharma, the first-year intern.
- **Hideout:** Library, Stack 7 (an encrypted USB drive inside the library's copy of *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning*).
- **Recovery:** Seize the drive and decrypt it (passphrase: BUZZKILL).

## How the clues lead there

**From the briefing (public):** the door opened with a keycard swipe at 2:13 AM; the weights were copied at 2:31 AM; the door locked at 2:47 AM.

**Suspect cards (shown at the briefing):** Rohan's keycard was **revoked** after his ban. Sid has an **active** Lab 404 student card. Meera's card only opens the ByteForce lab. The guard uses physical keys. HIVE-BOT is software.

**Clue 1 · Round 1 (the four real leads):**
- A **student** keycard opened the door → rules out Prof. Vector, Bhaskar Anna and HIVE-BOT.
- **Zero bytes** left the network → nothing was uploaded: rules out *The cloud*, *Revoke the cloud token* and *Trigger the watermark* (that only works on a copy running online).
- Snapshots were **off** → rules out *Roll back the server*.
- A student bought a **USB drive** at 10 PM → it walked out physically.
- ⇒ The method can only be **Seize the drive and decrypt it**.

**Clue 2 · Round 2 (the decoded log):** the notes spell **LIBRARY**; the sealed line reads **SHELF SEVEN. PASSPHRASE BUZZKILL** → the hideout is **Library, Stack 7**, and the drive is encrypted, which confirms the method.

**Clue 3 · Round 3 (the Lineup):** the real alibis clear Prof. Vector (flight), Meera (Hyderabad), Bhaskar (security register) and HIVE-BOT (offline). Two more fakes tried to frame Vector and ByteForce. Only **Rohan and Sid** faked their own alibis.

**The last step:** Rohan's keycard was revoked, but a working student card opened the door → **Sid**. Careful crews get there without help.

**Bonus clue · Vault winners only:** the 2:13 AM card was issued this August to a **first-year** → confirms Sid. Crews without it have to trust the keycard logic, which is why the final bet gets interesting.
